the song

A hopeful Hindi alt-pop song about letting your guard down and searching, honestly, for something bigger than yourself.

TISHNAGI means thirst. Here it is a thirst for the truth: the song is about being vulnerable, being honest with yourself, and reaching for a higher power when you can't find the answers alone. The production keeps it bright and hopeful. Midway through, the song shifts into a slower second half in a new key, carried by the same hook.

for fans of Bleachers, Frank Ocean, FINNEAS.

about

Timothy Thampy is a producer and songwriter based in Mumbai. Since 2022 he has produced more than 40 records, working with artists including Janisht Joshi, Raghav Meattle, Phosphenes, sudan, BENDI, Sagar Verma and Utsavi Jha.

His recent work includes “Parchhaiyan” and “Ab Na Laut Paayenge” from Talkhiyaan, released through Sony Music India. Under his own name he has released the EP songs for nora and the single “5 o clock”, both in 2023. TISHNAGI is written, produced and sung by him, and mixed and mastered by pixlpxl®.
